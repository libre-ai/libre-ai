# Big Bang roadmap

1. **Cleanup — complete** — preserve, reconcile, verify and archive legacy repositories.
2. **Specification Lock — complete** — ADR-0003 is closed and all 85 catalog authorities are locked; contract promotion grants no product or release authority.
3. **Foundation Build — current** — close Notebook Core v2 Gate B by proving real browser-process OOM handling on the exact product host across Chromium, Firefox and WebKit. The qualified 32+ Gio class is the sole required hardware class; optional 8 Gio and 16–24 Gio contributions may extend future support but cannot block this gate. Qualification uses public fixtures only; user backups and release remain blocked.
4. **Parallel Reconstruction** — apps and capabilities in final paths after their explicit upstream implementation controls.
5. **Global Hardening** — security, accessibility, data, operations and release candidate.
6. **Single Cutover** — configure Clever Cloud, deploy, switch public surfaces and archive legacy permanently.
7. **Distribution** — projections, packages, training and independent reproduction.

Progress is tracked by gates in [`GOALS.md`](GOALS.md), not by dates or issue count.
