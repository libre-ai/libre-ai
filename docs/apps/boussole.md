# Boussole

- **Path:** `apps/boussole`
- **Purpose:** compare local priorities with sourced public votes without profiling.
- **Runtime:** Bun/React local-first.
- **Owns:** questionnaire, public datasets, local responses and result presentation.
- **Rust owns:** deterministic scoring if the methodology remains in Libre AI.
- **Critical gates:** no transmission of positions, symmetric wording, reproducible calculation, abstention visibility, methodological/legal review and accessibility.
- **Strategic role:** stress-test local-first, explainability, provenance and methodological neutrality in a sensitive public-interest domain.
